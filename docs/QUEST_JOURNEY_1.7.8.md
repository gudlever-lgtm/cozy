# Quest journal and connected journey — 1.7.8

The journal has illustrated character tabs and two bounded quest cards per character. Details explain the story, every objective, prerequisites and exact reward materials. Ready quests claim through the existing idempotent ledger; locked quests lead to their prerequisite. Active objectives route to the matching mini-game hub, resource gathering or the correct decoration page. Entering these screens preserves the active Solitaire deal and does not silently replace an unfinished mini-game.

Secondary screens share a compact journey ribbon with actual quest objective/claim, area, story and building milestones. It links to the next initial quest and appearance controls. This is a read-only presentation calculation, not a new currency or reward system. The ribbon is omitted on the garden, Solitaire board and active mini-games to preserve play space.

Inventory uses a compact five-resource strip and four owned items per page. Mini-games have an illustrated bounded hub, difficulty controls and explicit resume. Their longer instructions open on demand. Settings have four compact sections with their own bounded scrollbar. The original seasonal scenes, native ambient sprites, resource atlas and character portraits are reused; no new placeholder emojis replace supplied art. Secondary-page surfaces expose more of the garden. World particles appear sooner and slightly larger/brighter, retaining sparse densities and seasonal species. Summer fireflies are available through manual season selection; saved season, animation-off, reduced-motion and background policies remain authoritative.
