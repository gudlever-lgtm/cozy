**Title:** Cozy Solitaire

**Short description:** Enjoy gentle Solitaire, help animal friends, and grow a little garden of your own.

**Description:** Settle into classic Solitaire and a world of small joys. Play draw-one or draw-three Klondike, explore the seasonal cottage garden, and help Millie, Marmalade and Pip along their stories. Earn Glimmers through play and mini-games, improve buildings, and open paths into the forest, riverside, village and winter garden. Garden Match, Hidden Treasures and Tea Time can be replayed without limits. Choose Danish or English and continue offline with a local profile. No ads, payments or mandatory online account.
