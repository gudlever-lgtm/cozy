# Development roadmap

The canonical design is `COZY_MASTER_GAME_DESIGN.md`. The uploaded execution brief adds the complete V1.0–V2.0 roadmap. Android/iOS only; no advertising, purchases, energy gates, mandatory accounts or time-limited essential content.

1. Preserve V1.0–V1.7 systems and regressions.
2. Add versioned saves for unfinished mini-games and backup recovery.
3. Add deterministic Solitaire challenges and additional mini-game levels.
4. Implement Enchanted Forest, Riverside Garden, Cozy Village and Winter Wonderland, with purchases, buildings, NPC relationships, extended quests and evergreen seasonal festivals.
5. Integrate original vector scenes/card themes and lightweight feedback with motion/audio controls.
6. Prepare DA/EN store copy, privacy information, release notes and explicit build/testing checklist.
7. Verify native bundles and available APK compilation. EAS production jobs, iOS signing and physical-device QA remain external prerequisites unless credentials/devices become available.

Status and evidence are maintained in `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` and `TEST_RESULTS.md`. Source and tests, not version labels, establish completion.
